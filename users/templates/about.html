Our Mission

Redefining the Way You Explore India

At HeritageGuide, our motto is to make more people aware of unheard and unseen monuments and museums that represent India's incredible history. We believe every heritage site has a story worth telling.

Discover Hidden Gems

We spotlight lesser-known heritage sites alongside iconic landmarks, giving every monument the attention it deserves.

AI-Powered Guidance

Smart Itineraries

Our Journey

How HeritageGuide Was Built

January 2025

Idea & Research

The concept was born -- a platform to make India's heritage accessible. We researched heritage sites, user needs, and the technology stack.

February 2025

Design & Frontend

Built the frontend with HTML, CSS, and Django templates. Designed the UI with a focus on clean, modern aesthetics and mobile responsiveness.

March 2025

Backend & Database

Integrated Django backend with SQLite, then migrated to Supabase PostgreSQL. Built the booking system, itinerary planner, and AI chatbot.

May 2026

Launch on Vercel

Deployed HeritageGuide on Vercel with 45+ attractions across 4 cities, full slot booking, AI guidance, and a beautiful redesigned frontend.
